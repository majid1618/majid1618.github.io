Privacy Policy

Pacyrinth

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Pacyrinth handles information in connection with the mobile game and related services offered by the developer. We aim to keep the game simple, privacy-conscious, and transparent.

Pacyrinth is designed primarily to run locally on your device. The game stores user preferences, save data, and gameplay progress on the device for gameplay functionality and to improve the user experience. We do not require an account to play.

2. Information We Collect

  • Gameplay data stored locally on your device such as scores, bank balance, unlocked stages, settings, name preferences, leaderboard entries, and audio/haptics choices.
  • Advertising data when advertisements are displayed through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers, device information, app usage information, diagnostic information, and, where applicable, coarse location or device identifiers to show relevant ads and support ad measurement.
  • Device and app diagnostics including OS version, language, device model, app version, and crash/error information, as required by third-party service providers or app stores.
  • Contact information only if you choose to contact us directly through email or other support channels.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve gameplay functionality.
  • To save preferences, progress, leaderboard data, and bank/progression information locally on the device.
  • To show ads through Google AdMob and measure ad performance.
  • To support analytics, crash reporting, app stability, and technical troubleshooting.
  • To respond to support or privacy inquiries when you contact us.

4. Local Storage and Data Ownership

The game may store data locally on the device in app storage. This includes progress, preferences, and saved game state. This information remains on the device unless you choose to share it with us or it is transmitted by a third-party service (such as an ad network or app platform).

We do not sell personal information. We do not require account creation, and we do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data such as government IDs, payment information, or private photos.

5. Third-Party Services

Pacyrinth may use Google AdMob for advertising, and Google may process data in accordance with its own Privacy Policy. Please review Google’s privacy terms for details about the data it collects and how it is used. If Google Play Services or other platform services are used, their privacy policies also apply.

We use these services to support advertising, app functionality, analytics, and serving the game.

6. Children’s Privacy

Pacyrinth is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted information, they should contact us and we will take steps to remove it and stop processing it.

7. Data Retention

We retain locally stored gameplay data only as long as necessary for the game to function and for user convenience. Advertising-related data retained by Google AdMob is subject to Google’s retention schedules and privacy policies. If you delete the app or clear app data, local game data may be removed from the device.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect device-side data and rely on the security of the underlying mobile platform and third-party services. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection against all risks.

9. Your Choices

  • You can disable or reset app data through your device’s app settings.
  • You can limit ad personalization settings in your device or Google account settings.
  • You can contact us if you want to request deletion or clarification of any data you have shared directly with us.
